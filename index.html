About The Club

The ASSU Debate Club provides students with opportunities to develop public speaking, research, and critical thinking skills through structured debates, competitions, and workshops.

Our Goals & Values

Critical Thinking

We challenge assumptions and develop well-reasoned positions on complex issues.

Respectful Discourse

We maintain an environment where diverse perspectives are heard and discussed with civility.

Leadership

We develop confident communicators who can articulate ideas effectively.
