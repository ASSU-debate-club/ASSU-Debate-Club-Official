About The Club

What We Do

The ASSU Debate Club provides students with opportunities to develop public speaking, research, and critical thinking skills through structured debates, competitions, and workshops.

We participate in local and regional tournaments, host guest speakers from various fields, and organize internal mock debates and lectures to sharpen our skills.

Why Join?

  • Develop confidence in public speaking
  • Enhance critical thinking and argumentation skills
  • Build teamwork and leadership abilities
  • Compete in regional tournaments

Our Goals & Values

Critical Thinking

We challenge assumptions, analyze arguments, and develop well-reasoned positions on complex issues.

Respectful Discourse

We maintain an environment where diverse perspectives are heard and discussed with civility and openness.

Leadership

We develop confident communicators who can articulate ideas effectively and inspire others through thoughtful debate.
